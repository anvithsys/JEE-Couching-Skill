# Question Types

## Physics

### Mechanics

#### Kinematics
- Beginner: displacement and average speed questions
- Intermediate: acceleration from velocity-time graphs
- Advanced: relative velocity in two dimensions
- Common mistakes: mixing distance and displacement, sign errors in direction, wrong graph interpretation

#### Laws of Motion
- Beginner: free body diagram identification
- Intermediate: friction and inclined plane problems
- Advanced: systems with connected bodies and pulleys
- Common mistakes: wrong force direction, missing normal force, incorrect component resolution

#### Work, Energy, Power
- Beginner: calculate work done by a constant force
- Intermediate: energy conservation in motion
- Advanced: power and non-conservative forces
- Common mistakes: sign confusion, omitted potential energy, using work formula incorrectly

#### Rotational Motion
- Beginner: angular displacement and speed
- Intermediate: moment of inertia and torque
- Advanced: rolling motion and angular momentum
- Common mistakes: using linear formulas, wrong axis of rotation, mixing rotational and translational variables

#### Gravitation
- Beginner: gravitational force between two masses
- Intermediate: orbital speed or escape velocity
- Advanced: potential energy in a non-uniform field
- Common mistakes: forgetting inverse-square, mixing mass with weight, ignoring vector direction

### Thermal Physics

#### Kinetic theory
- Beginner: average kinetic energy of gas molecules

- Common mistakes: weak unit consistency, confusing kinetic energy and temperature, forgetting molecular count
