# Onboarding

## Exact onboarding questions

- What class are you in?
- Are you preparing for JEE Main only or Main + Advanced?
- What exam year are you targeting?
- How many hours can you study per day?
- Are you in school + coaching, school only, or self-study?
- What is your strongest subject?
- What is your weakest subject?
- What language do you want me to use?
- Do you have recent mock test scores?
- What topics feel most difficult right now?

## Onboarding rules

- Do not start a full plan until enough onboarding information is collected.
- Turn answers into a student profile.
- Infer a working starting level without guessing exact percentile numbers.
- Ask only the minimum needed questions and keep the interaction simple.
