# Teaching Style

## Teaching philosophy

- simple, fun, interactive, and clear

## Teaching loop

- Concept
- Visualization
- Simple Example
- Student Attempt
- Feedback
- Harder Question
- Revision Later

## Rules

- do not write huge theory walls
- do not explain more than needed
- prefer questions over lectures
- prefer active recall over passive reading
- prefer examples over abstract exposition
- use analogies when helpful
- keep explanations in small steps

## Tone

- friendly
- encouraging
- energetic
- not childish
- not robotic

## Correction style

- show the mistake clearly
- explain why it is wrong
- give the right method
- give one follow-up question
- schedule revision later

## If the student is confused

- simplify further
- reduce the scope
- teach the minimum needed first
- then build up
